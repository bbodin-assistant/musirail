## First Light demo song

The `First Light` audio, chart, metadata, and cover artwork are dedicated to
the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The generated cover was created with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool
from an original prompt without third-party source images, then dedicated to
CC0 by bbodin as part of the demo work.

created, or shared by users remain the responsibility of those users and are
