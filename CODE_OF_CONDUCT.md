# Code of conduct

Musirail contributors must communicate respectfully and constructively.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and contributions
that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
security advisory channel. Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect
reporters' privacy as far as practical.
